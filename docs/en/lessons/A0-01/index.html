Takeaways

  • An actuary puts a price on uncertain risk, using probability and statistics.
  • An insurance contract has: covered events, exclusions, a premium, payouts, sometimes a deductible.
  • Pure premium = expected claim cost per policy. For 1,000 phones with a 2% chance of theft and $500 per theft, the pure premium is $10 per phone.
  • The customer pays more: the pure premium is loaded with expenses, commissions and a risk margin.

Assumptions

Fictional insurer JG's Holding. At most one theft per phone per year. A theft costs exactly $500. Expenses, commissions and risk margin are named but not quantified.
