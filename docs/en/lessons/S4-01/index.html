Learning sheet · Level 1, batch 4 · S4.01

Three readings of a probability.

“Seventy percent chance of rain tomorrow”: what does it really mean? With a fair die, a long series of “seventy-percent” days and an imaginary expert, we explore three readings of probability: symmetry, long-run frequency and degree of confidence. We express them as fractions, decimals and percentages.

Learning objectives

  • Tell apart three readings of probability: symmetry, long-run frequency and degree of confidence
  • Write a probability as a fraction, decimal and percentage
