The top, the bottom and the middle: quartiles and box plots

The median cuts a data set in two. Quartiles cut it in four, deciles in ten. We work out the quartiles of eleven commuting times by hand, build a box plot, and learn to read a D9/D1 ratio without mixing up a value and a group. Worked exercise at the end. Fictional examples, checked calculations. Series "Reading numbers without being fooled".

⏱ 3 min read✎ Written by JG↻ Updated 2026-11-09
Videos · Video in preparation

Learning objectives

  • Find the median and quartiles of a small data set
  • Read and build a box plot
  • Interpret a D9/D1 interdecile ratio without mixing up a value and a group

Prerequisites

  • S2.05: mean or median

The top, the bottom, the middle: how do you describe them?

The median tells you where the middle is. But how do you describe the bottom, the top, and everything in between?

Cutting in four

Let's sort the values, then cut them like a cake.

Eleven journeys, sorted (in minutes)

Here are eleven commuting times, sorted from shortest to longest. The median is the sixth value: eighteen minutes. The first quartile, Q1, is the middle of the five values below it: ten minutes. The third quartile, Q3, is the middle of the five values above it: twenty-five minutes.

  • 5 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 15 · 18 · 20 · 22 · 25 · 30 · 45
  • Median: the 6th value, 18 min
  • Q1: middle of the lower half, 10 min
  • Q3: middle of the upper half, 25 min

Half-series method: with an odd number of values, the median belongs to neither half.

Four slices of a quarter each

The quartiles cut the data into four slices of roughly a quarter each. About a quarter of the journeys take ten minutes or less: here, three out of eleven. The middle half takes between ten and twenty-five minutes. The width of that middle half, twenty-five minus ten, is called the interquartile range: fifteen minutes.

  • About a quarter of journeys: 10 min or less
  • The middle half: between 10 and 25 min
  • Interquartile range: 25 − 10 = 15 min

The box plot

These five numbers can be drawn as a single picture.

Min, Q1, median, Q3, max: one box

The box runs from the first to the third quartile: it holds the middle half. The line inside the box is the median. The whiskers stretch out to the smallest and the largest value. The top whisker is long: a few journeys take much longer than the rest.

Here the whiskers run from minimum to maximum. A variant shows outliers separately (coming lesson).

The box shows the centre, the spread and the skew

At a glance, the box shows the centre, the spread, and the skew: the median sits nearer the bottom of the box, and the top whisker is the longest.

Deciles and the D9/D1 ratio

And what if we cut in ten instead of four?

Deciles cut in ten

Deciles cut the sorted data into ten equal parts. The first decile, D1, has ten percent of the values below it. The ninth decile, D9, has ten percent of the values above it. Their ratio, D9 over D1, measures the gap between the top and the bottom: statistics offices publish it for living standards.

  • D1: 10% of values lie below it
  • D9: 10% of values lie above it
  • D9/D1: how many times the top exceeds the bottom

An imaginary country

In an imaginary country, D1 is one thousand euros a month and D9 is three thousand four hundred. The ratio is three point four: the entry point to the richest ten percent is three point four times the ceiling of the poorest ten percent.

\[\dfrac{D9}{D1}=\dfrac{3{,}400}{1{,}000}=3.4\]

D9 is a value, not a group

Watch out for the trap: D9 is a value, a boundary. It is not the income of the richest ten percent, who all earn at least D9, and often far more.

Exercise

Box: min 2, Q1 6, median 9, Q3 14, max 20. Median? Interquartile range? Share of values between 6 and 14?

Your turn. A box plot shows: minimum two, first quartile six, median nine, third quartile fourteen, maximum twenty. What is the median? The interquartile range? And what share of the values lies between six and fourteen?

Solution

Let's see. The median is the line in the box: nine. The interquartile range is fourteen minus six: eight. Between the first and third quartiles lies the middle half: about fifty percent of the values.

  • Median: the line in the box, 9
  • Interquartile range: 14 − 6 = 8
  • Between Q1 and Q3: half the values
Answer : 9; 8; about 50%

Key points

Key points. Quartiles cut the sorted data in four. The box plot shows the middle half, with the median. And a decile is a boundary, not a group.

  • Quartiles: the sorted data cut in four
  • The box: the middle half, median inside
  • D9 is a boundary, not a group
