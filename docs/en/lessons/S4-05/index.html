Learning sheet · Level 1, batch 4 · S4.05

Events: complement, union, intersection.

At least one six in four die rolls: how likely is it? With an imaginary Venn diagram and a historical problem put to Pascal and Fermat by the Chevalier de Méré, we learn to handle events. The complement turns “at least one” into “none”, and the union formula avoids double counting.

Learning objectives

  • Represent events with a Venn diagram
  • Use the complement and the union formula
  • Identify incompatible events
