A dataset from start to finish: the full workflow.

A table of numbers lands on your desk. What do you do? With a fictional case study — monthly births in an imaginary region — we walk through the full workflow: ask a question, check provenance, clean, describe, chart, state limitations and conclude in three honest sentences.

Learning objectives

  • Apply the full workflow: question, provenance, cleaning, description, chart, limitations and conclusion
  • Write an honest three-sentence conclusion
