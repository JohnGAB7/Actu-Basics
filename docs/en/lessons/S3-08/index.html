Learning sheet · Level 1, batch 4 · S3.08

Indices and moving averages: reading change over time.

How can we compare changes in bread prices and rent since 2000? With two imaginary series, we use the base-100 index to compare paths. Then we smooth a fluctuating monthly series with a moving average to reveal its trend. Worked exercise at the end.

Learning objectives

  • Compute a base-100 index to compare two series
  • Smooth a series with a moving average
  • Tell short-term fluctuations apart from a series' trend
