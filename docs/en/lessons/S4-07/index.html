Learning sheet · Level 1, batch 4 · S4.07

Conditional probability.

Does knowing it is raining change the probability of a traffic jam? With a cross-table of one hundred imaginary days, we explore conditional probability: conditioning means restricting the sample space to cases where the hypothesis holds. We also learn not to confuse P(A | B) with P(B | A).

Learning objectives

  • Compute P(A | B) in a cross-table
  • Tell apart P(A | B) and P(B | A)
