## Calculs recalculés par la machine

- Moyenne, ligne 1 : `(4 + 5 + 5 + 6) / 4` = 5
- Moyenne, ligne 2 : `(1 + 3 + 7 + 9) / 4` = 5
- Somme des écarts, ligne 2 : `(1 - 5) + (3 - 5) + (7 - 5) + (9 - 5)` =
  0
- Variance, ligne 2 :
  `((1 - 5)^2 + (3 - 5)^2 + (7 - 5)^2 + (9 - 5)^2) / 4` = 10
- Écart-type, ligne 2 : `sqrt(10)` = 3.16
- Variance, ligne 1 :
  `((4 - 5)^2 + (5 - 5)^2 + (5 - 5)^2 + (6 - 5)^2) / 4` = 0.5
- Écart-type, ligne 1 : `sqrt(0.5)` = 0.71
- Moyenne : `(2 + 4 + 4 + 6) / 4` = 4
- Somme des carrés des écarts :
  `(2 - 4)^2 + (4 - 4)^2 + (4 - 4)^2 + (6 - 4)^2` = 8
- Écart-type, division par n : `sqrt(8 / 4)` = 1.41
- Variance, division par n − 1 : `8 / 3` = 2.67
- Écart-type, division par n − 1 : `sqrt(8 / 3)` = 1.63

## Description EN

Two bus lines both make you wait 5 minutes on average. On one, you
always wait about 5 minutes; on the other, sometimes 1, sometimes 9. How
do you capture that difference in a single number? We compute the
variance and standard deviation by hand, see why the deviations are
squared, and what "a standard deviation of 3 minutes" means. Worked
exercise at the end. Fictional examples, checked calculations. Series
"Reading numbers without being fooled".
