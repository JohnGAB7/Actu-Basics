## Calculs recalculés par la machine

- Conducteurs : `98000 + 2000` = 100000
- Accidents : `400 + 100` = 500
- Part des sobres parmi les accidents, en % : `100 × 400 / 500` = 80
- Sobres sans accident : `98000 - 400` = 97600
- Ayant bu, sans accident : `2000 - 100` = 1900
- Risque d'accident des sobres, en % : `100 × 400 / 98000` = 0.41
- Risque d'accident après avoir bu, en % : `100 × 100 / 2000` = 5
- Rapport des risques : `(100 / 2000) / (400 / 98000)` = 12
- Part des fumeurs qui toussent, en % : `100 × 60 / 200` = 30
- Part des non-fumeurs qui toussent, en % : `100 × 80 / 800` = 10
- Part de fumeurs parmi ceux qui toussent, en % : `100 × 60 / (60 + 80)`
  = 43

## Description EN

"80% of crashes involve a driver who hadn't been drinking." Does that
mean drinking protects you? Of course not: almost all drivers are sober.
With a made-up cross-tab of 100,000 drivers, we learn to compute row and
column percentages, and to pick the right denominator. Worked exercise
at the end. Fictional examples, checked calculations. Series "Reading
numbers without being fooled".
