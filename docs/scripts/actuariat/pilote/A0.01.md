<td>This ten dollars is called the pure premium. In this simplified
example, it is the probability of a theft, p, times what the insurer
pays if it happens, C: zero point zero two times five hundred, which
gives ten dollars. In general, the pure premium is the expected claim
cost per policy, with nothing added.</td>

It may pay commissions to the shops or brokers that sell the contract.
It needs a margin for uncertainty, because twenty thefts is only an
