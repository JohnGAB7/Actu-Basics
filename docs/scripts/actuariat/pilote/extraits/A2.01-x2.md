## Calculs recalculés par la machine

- Pure premium: `0.02 × 500` = 10
- Premium charged: `10 + 1` = 11
- Premiums collected, 100 phones: `100 × 11` = 1100
- Premiums collected, 10,000 phones: `10000 × 11` = 110000
- Smallest number of thefts exceeding premiums, 100 phones:
  `floor(100 × 11 / 500) + 1` = 3
- Smallest number of thefts exceeding premiums, 10,000 phones:
  `floor(10000 × 11 / 500) + 1` = 221
- P(3 thefts or more out of 100), in %:
  `100 × (1 - sum(comb(100, k) × 0.02^k × 0.98^(100 - k) for k in range(3)))`
  = 32
- P(221 thefts or more out of 10,000), in %:
  `100 × (1 - sum(exp(log(comb(10000, k)) + k × log(0.02) + (10000 - k) × log(0.98)) for k in range(221)))`
  = 7.3
