## Calculs recalculés par la machine

- Simple interest per year: `0.05 × 1000` = 50
- Simple interest over 30 years: `30 × 0.05 × 1000` = 1500
- Total with simple interest: `1000 × (1 + 0.05 × 30)` = 2500
- Future value after 30 years at 5%: `1000 × 1.05^30` = 4322
- Rule of 72 at 5%: `72 / 5` = 14.4
- Exact doubling time at 5%: `log(2) / log(1.05)` = 14.2
