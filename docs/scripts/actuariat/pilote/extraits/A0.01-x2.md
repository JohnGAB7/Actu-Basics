## Description EN (YouTube)

A fictional insurer, JG's Holding, insures 1,000 phones: a 2% chance of
theft, \$500 per stolen phone, a pure premium of \$10. It adds a \$100
deductible. What is the new pure premium? Your turn, then the answer. An
extract from the first lesson of our actuarial science series,
"Actuaries: how maths helps make decisions under risk", watch the full
lesson on the channel. Made-up figures, checked calculations.

\#actuarialscience \#insurance \#deductible

## Calculs recalculés par la machine

- Pure premium without deductible: `0.02 × 500` = 10
- Payout after deductible: `500 - 100` = 400
- Pure premium with deductible: `0.02 × (500 - 100)` = 8
